Repeat Reminders

Interval alarms for Android. Set a start time and Repeat Reminders rings again every few seconds, minutes or hours until the time you choose, on the days you choose.

Features

  • Any repeat interval, ending at midnight, at a set time or after a number of rings
  • Days of the week, days of the month, calendar dates, months and years
  • Custom ring duration, gradual volume and an optional full-screen alarm
  • Built-in beeps, phone ringtones or your own audio files
  • Next alarm in the notification bar and a history of past alarms
  • Light, dark and black themes, 12 or 24-hour clock

Support

Questions, bugs or feature requests: sudhirkumarshahu80@gmail.com

Alarm late or not ringing? Open Repeat Reminders, go to Settings, then Permissions, and grant everything marked as required. On Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, OnePlus, Huawei and Samsung phones, also turn on Autostart and set Battery to "No restrictions" for Repeat Reminders.
