Repeat Reminders Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

This policy explains how the Repeat Reminders Android app ("the app"), published by Sudhir Kumar Shahu ("I", "me"), handles your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. Everything you create stays on your phone.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your phone, in its private storage:

  • Your alarms: names, times, intervals, days and sound choices
  • Alarm history for the last 90 days: when each alarm rang and whether it was dismissed or snoozed
  • Your settings, such as theme and time format

This data is never sent to me or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the history in the app, deleting alarms, or uninstalling the app.

Device backup

If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. Google handles that backup under your Google account and the Google Privacy Policy. I cannot access it.

Audio files

If you choose a song or audio file as an alarm sound, the app gets permission to read that one file so it can play it. The app does not scan, upload or copy your files.

Pictures

If you add a picture to a reminder, you pick it with Android's photo picker. The app saves a resized copy of only that picture inside its own storage so it can show it when the reminder rings. The copy is deleted when you remove the picture or delete the reminder.

Birthday import

If you choose to import birthdays, the app reads them on your phone from the calendars synced to it, or from a calendar (.ics) or contacts (.vcf) file you pick or share to the app. Only the names and dates of the birthdays you tick are saved, as reminders. Nothing is uploaded, and the app does not change your calendars or contacts.

Permissions and why they are used

PermissionWhy
NotificationsTo show ringing alarms, the next alarm and the alarm log
Alarms and reminders (exact alarms)To ring at the exact time you set
Run at startupTo set your alarms again after the phone restarts
Foreground service, wake lock, vibrationTo keep playing the alarm sound and vibration while it rings
Full-screen notifications, display over other appsTo show the optional alarm screen, including on the lock screen, and the pop-up card over other apps
Calendar, read only (optional)Only if you import birthdays from your calendars, to find birthday events
Do Not Disturb access (optional)Only if you turn on "Ring through Do Not Disturb", to let the alarm be heard and then restore your setting
Ignore battery optimisation (optional)So the system does not delay or stop your alarms

The app does not use the internet, your location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone number, and it never reads your calendars unless you start a birthday import. To tell whether you are in a call, it checks only whether the phone's audio is in call mode. It does not read call details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
